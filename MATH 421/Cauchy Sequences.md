## 2.10 Monotone Sequences and Cauchy Sequences

**10.8 Definition**
* ***Cauchy Sequence***: A sequence of real numbers ($s_{n}$) is Cauchy if:
	for each $\varepsilon >0$ there exists a number $N$ such that $m,n > N$ implies $| s_n -s_m| < \varepsilon$.

**10.9 Lemma**
	*Convergent sequences are Cauchy sequences*.

**10.10 Lemma**
	Cauchy sequences are bounded.

**10.11 Theorem**
	A sequence is a convergent sequence *if and only if* it is a Cauchy sequence
