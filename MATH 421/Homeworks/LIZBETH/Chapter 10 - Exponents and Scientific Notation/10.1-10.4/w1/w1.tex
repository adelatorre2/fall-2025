\textbf{A. Write the product using exponents (Section 10.1).}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]

\item $5\cdot 5\cdot 5\cdot n\cdot n$

\item $x\cdot x\cdot y\cdot y\cdot y$

\item $b\cdot b\cdot b\cdot b\cdot c\cdot c$
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{F. Applications (mixed 10.1--10.4).}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., resume]
\item A bacterium is $1800$ micrometers long. If $1$ micrometer $=10^{-6}$ meters, what is the length in meters?

\item A value is multiplied by $10^5$ and then divided by $10^2$. Overall, what power of 10 is the value multiplied by?

\item A factory produces $6\times10^3$ items per day. How many items are produced in $2\times10^2$ days? (Write your answer in standard form.)
\end{enumerate}
